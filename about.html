About Us

We find faces. We build careers.

Frame Talent Models is a talent management agency built around one simple idea — everyone deserves a shot in front of the camera. We put out open calls, scout new faces and give people who've never modeled before a real chance to perform.

Once you're in our books, we connect you directly with brands running product campaigns, digital ads and business promotions. You bring the face; we bring the opportunity — and handle everything in between, from your first application to the day you're on set.
